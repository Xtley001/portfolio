# Gyrfalcon: Multi-Protocol Solana Liquidation Engine

**Gyrfalcon** is a sub-millisecond Solana liquidation engine architected around high-frequency gRPC state feeds, in-process SVM simulation, and multi-region Jito bundle submission.

## Kamino Lend Primary Focus

Gyrfalcon anchors on **Kamino Lend**, modeled at **$1.34B supplied and $1.03B borrowed** (76.78% utilization):
- **Main Market:** SOL/USDC (80% LTV / 85% liquidation threshold)
- **JitoSOL/SOL Pool:** Correlated liquidity (95% liquidation threshold)
- **Modular Adaptability:** Shared latency-optimized pipeline (Geyser ingestion, in-memory health book, flash-source router, in-process LiteSVM simulation) extends fault-isolated adapters to **Save (Solend)** and **MarginFi**.

## Microsecond Execution Stack

- **Yellowstone Geyser gRPC:** Streaming account update ingestion directly into an in-memory lock-free orderbook and obligation tracker.
- **Instruction-Introspection Flash Borrow:** Fee-free atomic principal via Kamino Lend Flash Borrow (12,000 CU) and Solend Flash Loans (15,000 CU).
- **LiteSVM In-Process Simulation:** Every opportunity simulated within the process memory space before submission to prevent failed-transaction penalties.
- **Multi-Region Dual Submission:** Bundles submitted across staked QUIC and Jito endpoints in Amsterdam, New York, Tokyo, and Frankfurt with dynamic CU budgeting and Address Lookup Table (ALT) compaction.
