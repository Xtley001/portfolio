# Kingfisher: Curve Stablecoin Flash-Loan Arbitrage on Arbitrum One

**Kingfisher** is a low-latency execution bot engineered specifically to exploit temporary pricing imbalances between Curve stablecoin pools on **Arbitrum One**, backed by atomic flash-loan liquidity from Aave V3.

## Mechanism & Structural Edge

- **Atomic Price Spread Capture:** Borrows flash-capital from Aave V3, captures StableSwap pricing deviations across imbalanced Curve pools, and atomically repays principal and fees in a single transaction.
- **On-Chain Profit Guarantees:** Solidity callback asserts `require(netProfit >= minProfit)`. If the transaction loses a sequencer race, it reverts cleanly on-chain, risking only negligible transaction gas without ever endangering principal.
- **Arbitrum Timeboost & Private Routing:** Exploits Arbitrum's single-sequencer architecture and Timeboost priority ordering, broadcasting pre-signed transactions directly to the lowest-latency sequencer endpoint without public mempool leakage.
- **Nitro Node Co-Location:** Interacts directly over IPC with a local Arbitrum Nitro node achieving sub-0.1ms block-header notification latency.
- **Hot-Reloadable Parameters:** Includes a zero-downtime control plane (`POST /params`) to adjust profit floors and sizing ceilings in production without stopping the service.
