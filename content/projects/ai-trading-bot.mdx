# AI Trading Bot: Sentiment-Driven Equity Strategy

An applied machine learning trading framework parsing live financial news headlines with FinBERT transformers to drive automated equity position sizing and risk-managed execution.

## Pipeline Architecture

- **Financial NLP Pipeline:** Ingests live financial headlines, applying ProsusAI FinBERT to classify sentiment polarity (positive, negative, neutral) with probabilistic confidence scoring.
- **Dynamic Sizing & Brackets:** Weights order allocation by sentiment strength and volatility, submitting bracket orders (automated stop-loss and take-profit) through the Alpaca Markets API.
- **Backtesting Harness:** Multi-year historical validation over Yahoo Finance price histories verifying edge over benchmark buy-and-hold returns.
