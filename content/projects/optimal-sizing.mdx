# Optimal Sizing: Published Rust Workspace for Profit-Maximizing Trade Sizing

**optimal-sizing** is a high-performance, chain-agnostic Rust library and multi-crate workspace for profit-maximizing trade sizing against Constant Product Market Maker (CPMM), StableSwap, Proactive Market Maker (PMM), Concentrated Liquidity, Balancer, DODO, and CryptoSwap curves.

## Core Problem & Engineering Philosophy

Most trade sizing code in the wild relies on naive grid search or unexamined bisection loops, offering zero mathematical assurance of whether a computed size is actually optimal. `optimal-sizing` answers that directly by replacing heuristic guesswork with provable derivations:

- **Constant-Product Sizing:** Solved in closed form and provably optimal (`ProvenOptimal`).
- **StableSwap Sizing:** Solved via Newton's method with stated convergence conditions and invariant memoization (`NumericallyGuaranteed`).
- **Proactive Market Maker (PMM) Sizing:** Golden-section search conditional on an active runtime unimodality check (`f''(Δ) <= 0`) rather than assumed curvature.
- **Explicit Guarantee Tiers:** Every `SizingResult` carries a strongly-typed `GuaranteeTier` (`ProvenOptimal`, `NumericallyGuaranteed`, `EmpiricallyValidated`, or `ComposedFrom`), ensuring downstream execution engines never mistake heuristic estimates for proven mathematical bounds.

The workspace generalizes trade-sizing logic into standalone, reusable infrastructure across 8 dedicated crates:

## Curve Implementations & Optimization Mechanics

- **`Cpmm` (Constant Product / Uniswap v2):** Closed-form analytic solution with fixed gas and execution costs factored directly into the objective function.
- **`StableSwap` (Curve Invariant):** Solves the implicit Curve invariant via Newton's method. Invariant $D$ is memoized per invocation to prevent redundant re-solves (~1.5x measured speedup), paired with a $\sqrt{\text{tolerance}}$ finite-difference derivative step to eliminate numerical cancellation against the convergence noise floor.
- **`Pmm` (WooFi v2 Proactive Market Maker):** Golden-section search with dynamic unimodality checking that rejects non-concave intervals in the reverse regime. Search boundaries are dynamically derived from pool poles and quote-reserve capacity limits.
- **`ConcentratedLiquidity` (Uniswap v3):** Single-tick range sizing utilizing virtual reserves to achieve closed-form optimality (`ProvenOptimal`), verified against CPMM baselines.
- **`sizing-router` (Multi-Hop Heterogeneous Routing):** Joint multi-variable optimization over leg-1 input size across heterogeneous curves rather than post-facto per-leg composition. Reports `GuaranteeTier::ComposedFrom(Vec<GuaranteeTier>)`.

## Performance, Tooling & Verification

- **Live State Integration:** `sizing-integration` pulls live state from Uniswap v2 USDC/WETH and Curve 3pool on Ethereum mainnet, leveraging multicall batching to compress 10 RPC roundtrips down to 3.
- **Real-Time Daemon:** `sizing-watch` continuously streams updates and re-evaluates optimal capital allocation over active DEX liquidity pools.
- **Cross-Language Support:** First-class bindings for Python (PyO3) and WebAssembly (`wasm-bindgen`) enabling seamless execution in data pipelines and browser environments.
- **Strict Verification Contract:** Property-tested with `proptest`, zero-I/O boundary enforced on `sizing-core` via `cargo tree` audit, and CI enforced on every commit with `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings`.
