# Multi-Symbol VWAP & Volume Profile EA (MQL5)

An institutional-grade MetaTrader 5 trading system implementing Volume-Weighted Average Price (VWAP) and Volume Profile market microstructure indicators across multiple currency and commodity pairs simultaneously.

## Technical Capabilities

- **Institutional Microstructure Levels:** Calculates Point of Control (POC), High Volume Nodes (HVN), Low Volume Nodes (LVN), and Average Daily Range (ADR) across multiple active symbols.
- **R-Multiple Risk Management:** Dynamically trails trade risk from 1R to 4R, moving stops to breakeven upon achieving initial target milestones.
- **Economic Calendar News Filter:** Integrated calendar monitoring to suspend trade entries before high-impact geopolitical and macroeconomic announcements.
