# Skua: Atomic Flash-Loan Arbitrage for HyperEVM

**Skua** is an atomic flash-loan execution and arbitrage framework tailored specifically for **HyperEVM** (the EVM-compatible execution layer of the Hyperliquid L1, Chain ID 999).

## Four Core Strategies

1. **EVM to HyperCore Arbitrage:** Two-phase CoreWriter-mediated arbitrage bridging EVM spot prices with HyperCore perpetual orderbooks.
2. **HyperLend Liquidations:** Health-factor tracking and atomic liquidation execution on HyperLend pools.
3. **Triangular Arbitrage:** 3-hop atomic DEX cycles resolving pricing inefficiencies between newly launched HyperEVM AMMs.
4. **Stablecoin Depeg Capture:** Opportunistic arbitrage when pegged assets oscillate beyond fee boundaries.

## Architecture Highlights

- **Multi-Wallet Nonce Orchestration:** Concurrently manages 5 to 10 independent EOA wallets with 8 nonces each to bypass transaction queue serialization.
- **Gas-Priority Fast Path:** Optimized for `eth_sendRawTransactionSync` with golden-section search optimal sizing, tailored for HyperEVM's deterministic ordering without standard Flashbots auctions.
- **Fail-Safe Circuit Breakers:** Real-time rolling gas-spend kill switch and per-trade capital caps monitored by Axum telemetry and Prometheus metrics.
