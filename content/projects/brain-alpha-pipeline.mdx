# Brain-Alpha-Pipeline: WorldQuant BRAIN Research Automation

**Brain-Alpha-Pipeline** is an autonomous quantitative research and execution pipeline that continuously generates, screens, sweeps, and correlation-checks equity alpha candidates on the WorldQuant BRAIN platform. It serves as the live orchestration harness powered end-to-end by the modular [`brain-libraries`](/projects/brain-libraries) suite.

---

## Research Automation Architecture

- **Scheduled Strategy Engine:** Autonomous worker loop programmatically testing cross-sectional rankings, time-series decay operators, and multi-factor expressions across diverse datasets.
- **Integrated Orthogonal Decorrelation:** Automatically pipes candidate expressions through [`brain-decorrelator`](/projects/brain-libraries) to clear the platform's strict $|\rho| < 0.70$ correlation gatekeeper before running costly full simulations.
- **Dual-Mode Persistence:** Integrates with `brain-store` for PostgreSQL connection pooling and offline file fallbacks, tracking historical submissions and correlation matrices.
- **Fail-Loud Configuration:** Implements strict startup validation (`MissingConfigError`) ensuring API keys, database credentials, and risk boundaries are verified before execution.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards:** Features automated Telegram alerting with statistical performance summaries (Sharpe ratio, turnover, fitness score, margin) for human review and final submission approval.
