# Brain-Alpha-Pipeline: WorldQuant BRAIN Alpha Research Automation

**Brain-Alpha-Pipeline** is an autonomous quantitative research system that continuously generates, screens, sweeps, and correlation-checks equity alpha candidates on the WorldQuant BRAIN platform.

## Research Pipeline Architecture

- **Scheduled Strategy Engine:** Autonomous worker loop that programmatically tests mathematical operators, cross-sectional rankings, time-series decays, and multi-factor expressions.
- **Correlation & Overlap Checking:** Pre-screens candidate alphas against existing internal books and platform correlation limits, filtering out redundant signals prior to resource-heavy simulation sweeps.
- **Fail-Loud Configuration:** Implements strict startup validation (`MissingConfigError`) ensuring API keys, database credentials, and risk boundaries are verified before execution.
- **Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards:** Features automated Telegram alerting with statistical performance summaries (Sharpe ratio, turnover, fitness score, margin) for human review and final submission approval.
