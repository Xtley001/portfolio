# REACH: Field Operations & Outreach Management Platform

**REACH** is a distributed, mobile-first field-operations management platform designed for door-to-door canvassing, contact logging, and real-time coordinator analytics.

## System Architecture

- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Strict permissions dividing access among five tiers: Field Volunteers, Hub Coordinators, Directors, Registration Teams, and Decision Teams.
- **Invite-Only Provisioning:** Cryptographic invitation token workflows ensuring unauthorized actors cannot view voter or contact rolls.
- **Real-Time Dashboards:** High-performance analytics displaying coverage percentages, engagement sentiment, and geographic reach.
- **Full-Stack Stack:** Python 3.11 with FastAPI on the backend, React 18 frontend, and PostgreSQL persistent store.
