# Goshawk: Multi-Protocol Liquidation Engine for Ethereum Mainnet

**Goshawk** is a low-latency multi-protocol liquidation engine engineered for Ethereum Mainnet, monitoring and executing liquidations across six foundational lending protocols.

## Market Scope ($15.9B Active Debt)

Goshawk provides real-time state tracking across protocols representing **$15.9B in active borrowed debt** against **$27.2B in supplied TVL** (58.43% aggregate utilization):
1. **Aave V3:** $10.18B active debt via `liquidationCall`
2. **Morpho Blue:** $2.19B active debt via `liquidate`
3. **Spark:** $2.10B active debt
4. **Fluid:** $628M active debt via the Fluid Liquidity Layer
5. **Compound V3:** $581M active debt via `absorb`
6. **Euler V2:** $230M active debt via `liquidateVault`

## Gas Break-Even Economics & Regime Modeling

- **Dynamic Gas Regimes:** Operates adaptive minimum clip sizes based on network congestion regimes (Normal vs. Crash/Cascade Spike). During volatility spikes where gas costs range from $38.50 to $280 per tx, clip thresholds scale dynamically from $5,000 to $25,000.
- **Protocol Clear Thresholds:**
  - *Morpho Blue:* Clears at $900 debt (120k gas, 90% builder share, nets ~$396.40 on a $100k liquidation).
  - *Aave V3:* Clears at $2,700 debt (360k gas, nets ~$389.20 on a $100k liquidation).

## Liquidity Sourcing & MEV Pipeline

- **Zero-Fee Capital Sourcing:** Sourced fee-free from Morpho Blue Flash Loans, Balancer V2, and Spark/Maker DSR Flash, with fallback to Aave V3 FlashLoanSimple.
- **Oracle Pre-Filtering:** Chainlink price feeds (ETH/USD, BTC/USD, wstETH/ETH, USDC/USD, cbBTC/USD) monitored against deviation and heartbeat thresholds prior to block-level eligibility scans.
- **Builder Infrastructure:** Bundles formatted and submitted directly through Flashbots Relay (88% builder market share) with redundant low-latency submissions to Titan Builder and Beaver Build.
