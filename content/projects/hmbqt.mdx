# HMBQT: Hummingbird Quant v9 Solana Trading Vault

**HMBQT** is a multi-strategy quantitative order-flow trading vault built on **Ranger Earn** (Solana) executing perpetual futures strategies against **Drift Protocol**, engineered for the official Ranger Finance "Build-A-Bear" Hackathon 2026.

## Strategy Modules

- **Five Integrated Strategies:** Deploys statistical arbitrage, order-flow imbalance scalping, funding rate capture, basis trading, and momentum filters across Solana perps.
- **Real-Time Data Streaming:** Low-latency ingestion of L2 orderbooks and executed trade prints via Drift's decentralized orderbook (DLOB) WebSocket feeds without authentication bottleneck.
- **Helius RPC Execution:** Staked priority execution via Helius RPC nodes interfacing with Drift's on-chain programs.
- **Historical Backtesting Engine:** Backtested against high-resolution trade ticks sourced from Binance Vision aggTrade historical archives.
