# Shrike: Flash-Loan Cross-DEX Arbitrage Bot for Berachain

**Shrike** is an atomic flash-loan arbitrage searcher built for **Berachain** (Chain ID 80094), continuously scanning pool liquidity and executing multi-venue cycles through a dedicated owner-gated contract (`ShrikeArb.sol`).

## Strategy & Pricing Mathematics

- **Multi-DEX Coverage:** Actively tracks pools across Kodiak V3/V2, WooFi, BrownFi, and BEX.
- **Position Optimizer:** Combines AMM binary search over concentrated liquidity ticks with WooFi's Proactive Market Maker (PMM) algorithmic pricing equations to pinpoint profit-maximizing trade sizes.
- **On-Chain Slippage Enforcement:** Per-leg slippage controls and strict net-profit checks prevent miner exploitation and frontrunning.
- **Risk System:** Persistent SQLite transaction logging (`RevertTracker`), daily gas ceiling enforcement, and automatic circuit breakers on consecutive reverts.
